Healthy Exercise Habits

Start gradually

If you are new to exercise, begin with short, comfortable sessions and increase duration or intensity gradually as you feel able.

Be consistent

Walking, cycling, swimming, dancing, and strength exercises are possible options. Choose activities you enjoy and can maintain.

Make room for recovery

Balance activity with rest, adequate sleep, hydration, and meals that support your energy.

Respect your limits

Stop if you feel unwell. If you have an injury, persistent pain, or a health condition affecting exercise, seek appropriate professional advice.

Important: This planner provides general wellness information and does not replace medical advice. If you experience chest pain, fainting, or severe or unusual shortness of breath during exercise, stop and seek urgent medical assessment.
