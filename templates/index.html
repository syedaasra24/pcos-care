PCOS: Myths vs. Facts

A few reminders to help separate common misconceptions from evidence-based information.

Explore PCOS education
Myth & fact

Myth: PCOS only affects people who are overweight.

Fact: PCOS can affect people of different body sizes. Weight alone cannot confirm or rule it out.

Myth & fact

Myth: Irregular periods are the only sign of PCOS.

Fact: Symptoms vary. Some people experience acne or excess hair growth; others may have different symptoms.

Myth & fact

Myth: PCOS means someone cannot become pregnant.

Fact: PCOS can affect ovulation, but many people with PCOS do become pregnant, sometimes with treatment.
